## DURABLE — does not expire

**ONDS (Ondas Holdings).** Three segments: Ondas Networks (private wireless / FullMAX
SDR), Ondas Autonomous Systems (C-UAS, drone interception via Sentrycs, loitering
munitions), World View (high-altitude ISR). Acquisition stack: Mistral, Omnisys, World
View, Cyberhawk. Palantir partner. Peers: RCAT, KTOS, DPRO. Stated profitability
timeline OAS Q3 2027, company-wide Q1 2028 — a multi-year thesis, not an earnings play.
Dilution is structural, not one-time: acquisition consideration keeps arriving as resale
prospectuses, which creates a recurring supply overhang independent of the operating
business. That gap between business quality and share price is the whole ONDS story.

**SATS (EchoStar).** Holds a SpaceX equity stake via a spectrum-for-equity swap — an
actual equity claim, not narrative exposure. Legacy businesses (DISH, Hughes, Sling,
Boost) fund the option value. Preferred over CEF-wrapper proxies because there is no
premium-to-NAV risk and no sponsor distribution overhang.

**IONQ.** Trapped-ion quantum; hardware plus cloud access via the major clouds. DARPA
contract is government validation. SkyWater merger adds US foundry capability. Large
cash position means no dilution pressure for years — the balance sheet is the moat.

**XRP.** SEC/CFTC commodity classification confirmed 17 Mar 2026 — law, not narrative.
The $1.44–$1.50 band is a *structural* resistance: roughly 60% of circulating supply has
cost basis there, so it is supply, not a chart line, and it does not expire.

**ASTS.** Space-based cellular direct to unmodified handsets. Carrier JV alignment is
structural. Revenue depends on satellite ramp; launch cadence is the binary.

**AMC.** ~$3.9B long-term debt is the permanent bear anchor. Diversification into live
events reduces pure box-office dependency but does not change the debt.

**CAR (Avis).** $25B+ debt, near-$1B net loss. An ATM offering registered Mar 2026 is
the supply mechanism that deflates a squeeze. Fundamental bear case intact regardless of
squeeze dynamics.

**NVDA.** CUDA ecosystem lock-in is the largest switching cost in semis. China export
controls are structural, not resolvable quickly. **Documented behavioural pattern: NVDA
has a history of muted post-earnings moves despite large beats.** This is the fact that
Gate 3 exists for.

**VCX (Fundrise Innovation Fund).** Closed-end structure with no redemption mechanism,
so price is driven by narrative rather than NAV. The sponsor was systematically
registering share sales while retail bid the vehicle up. **The generalisable rule: when
the fund that created the vehicle is distributing at scale into retail demand, the
thesis is compromised regardless of the quality of the underlying assets.** Applies to
other CEFs and SPAC-like wrappers.
