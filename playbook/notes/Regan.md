loc.9    Dot-com-era success stories are survivorship; now sellers of courses.
loc.10   Author claims ~$5,000+/month in early years; shows a few of his own trade screenshots. (author claims)

loc.12   Trade only in narrow windows when setups and system agree.

loc.24   Course syllabus (sales).
loc.25–27 Author's 20 "golden rules" (paraphrase only):
   - follow the chart, not the news;
   - buy the first pullback after a new high / short the first bounce after a new low (AGREES R-001 spirit);
   - buy near support, sell near resistance, don't bet on breaks (AGREES R-007);
   - short rallies, not sell-offs (squeeze risk);
   - don't buy up into or short down into major moving averages; MA crossovers no longer work in stocks;
   - don't chase a stock that has run past the planned entry by more than a set amount (RULE candidate);

   - trade with the market's direction; when the index is down more than 2.5% on the day nearly all stocks move together → get out of penny stocks (RULE candidate);
   - double top/bottom failure trade; real breaks come on the 3rd/4th test (AGREES R-003);
   - last-hour trend tends to hold; avoid the first 5–15 minutes after the open (AGREES R-017 timing);
   - above the 200-day = bulls, below = bears; use it as a gauge, not a trigger (RULE candidate);
   - price remembers levels; giant volume often marks a trend change; tops are sharp, bottoms slow;
   - use a direct-access broker.
