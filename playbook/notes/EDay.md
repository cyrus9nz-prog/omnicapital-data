## loc.40–52
- loc.40: pins against a long trend (bearish pins in a strong uptrend) are invalid; trend context first. D.
- loc.40–42: ENGULFING (outside) bar = reversal; author calls them "extremely reliable" (no data); look for them only in ranges forming at the END of a trend; expect a move into a range, short hold; a counter-signal against the side gaining ground is invalid. D, chart example.
- loc.42: TWO-BAR REVERSAL = split pin; trade as continuation in small in-trend ranges; a very large one at a trend end can be traded as reversal. D.
- loc.43–45: range/trend framework: in-trend ranges are short/small; ranges grow as the trend ages (more countertrend participation); trend ends with a BUYING CLIMAX: huge bar on huge volume, then large engulfing/two-bar reversal; end-of-trend range = distribution. D.
- loc.45–49: TSLA H1 case Dec 2019–Feb 2020: ranges grew, rally driven by overnight gaps (smart money exiting), then climax: volume surge, >$100 overnight gap, bearish engulfing near the open → exit; author says chart showed it before Covid news. C (one case, hindsight).
- loc.49–51: trend progressing mainly via opening gaps = smart money gone; shallow angle = weak; steep early = strong; steep late after countertrend action = climax → don't enter, tighten/exit. Real strength = many large-bodied bars with small wicks + small in-trend ranges; growing ranges override strong bars. D.
- loc.51–52: ENTRY/STOP RULES for patterns (enter only in ranges within the trend analysis): inside bar → buy-stop beyond the large bar's break, stop beyond the smaller bar; pin → enter at market on close, stop beyond tail/wick; engulfing → enter on close, stop at 50% of the outside bar or beyond it; two-bar reversal → enter on close, stop at 50% of the two bars if large, beyond them if small. Exit at indicator signal or a preset risk multiple. D.
- loc.52: "unassisted": enter in a small in-trend range at market, stop beyond the range boundary (text says above resistance for a long — appears to be an error; not carried literally).
